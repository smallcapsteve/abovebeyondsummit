Featured Speakers

Voices Leading the Conversation

David Morgan

Founder & Publisher, The Morgan Report

David Morgan, widely known in the financial industry as the "Silver Guru," is a prominent precious metals analyst, author, and investor specializing in the silver market. He is the founder and publisher of The Morgan Report, a premium publication that helps independent investors, hedge funds, and mining companies navigate the commodity and precious metals sectors.

Steve Hyland

Founder, The Deep Dive

Steve Hyland, widely known across the North American financial community as "Small Cap Steve," is an investor, financial commentator, and the founder and primary face of The Deep Dive. By combining an analytical background with an approachable media presence, he has positioned The Deep Dive as a premier Canadian digital media platform for retail and junior stock market analysis.
